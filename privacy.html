Privacy

Current status: FantasyDraftTool is a private-development prototype and is not currently offered as a public consumer service.

Planned Yahoo integration

If Yahoo Fantasy connectivity is enabled, users will explicitly authorize read-only access through Yahoo OAuth. FantasyDraftTool intends to access only Yahoo Fantasy data needed to provide connected-league features, including league settings, scoring, rosters, teams/managers, standings, draft results, transactions, player ownership, matchups, and fantasy results where available.

Account credentials

FantasyDraftTool will not ask users for their Yahoo password. Yahoo authorization will occur through Yahoo's OAuth flow. OAuth credentials and tokens will not be published in this repository.

Use of league data

Authorized league data is intended to be used to provide league-specific fantasy football analysis and to maintain historical league intelligence for the user. FantasyDraftTool does not require Yahoo write access and is not intended to submit roster moves, waiver claims, trades, lineup changes, or draft selections.

Before public launch

If FantasyDraftTool is released publicly, this notice will be expanded into the production privacy policy covering retention, deletion, account disconnection, security, and contact information before accepting public users.

Fantasy data provided by Yahoo Fantasy where applicable.
